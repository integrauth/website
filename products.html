sunnahfast

Sunnah fasting reminders — email or push, the evening before, 7 PM your time, Insha Allah

Visit sunnahfast.integrauth.com
Free No Account Email + Push Installable PWA Hijri Calendar

Key Features

  • Reminders before Mondays & Thursdays, the White Days (13–15), Ashura, the first nine days of Dhul Hijjah incl. Arafah, and six of Shawwal — pausing respectfully for Ramadan, the Eids & Tashreeq
  • Two channels: email or push notifications — and sunnahfast installs as an app (PWA) straight from the browser, no app store
  • A short Jumuah email every Thursday evening — Surah al-Kahf, salawat & the hour of acceptance, Insha Allah
  • Lands at 7 PM in your own local timezone — the evening before, so suhoor is never a scramble
  • Umm al-Qura Hijri calendar with a ±1-day "sync with your local moon sighting" adjuster
  • Hijri ↔ Gregorian converter, month-by-month fasting-days pages, and a subscribable .ics calendar feed with built-in evening-before alarms for Google, Apple & Outlook
  • Private fasting journal (stored only on your device) and a free embeddable "next sunnah fast" widget for any website
  • Blog & reminder emails quote every hadith verbatim with its exact reference — machine-checked against verified source banks
  • Double opt-in signup and a genuine one-click unsubscribe — free forever, Insha Allah, no account, no card, ever

Notifications

Web Push — VAPID (RFC 8292) + RFC 8291 payload encryption, hand-built on WebCrypto

Resend (confirmation & evening reminders)

Protection

Cloudflare Turnstile, per-IP rate limiting

Scheduling

Hourly Cron Trigger, timezone-aware

Analytics
