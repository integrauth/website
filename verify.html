Verify a Certificate

Every IntegrAuth Academy certificate carries a unique ID. Enter it below to confirm it is in IntegrAuth Academy’s records, and see who it was issued to, their score, and the issue date.

It is printed near the bottom of the certificate and looks like IA-4KD7-9QX2-M3F8. Upper/lower case and the dashes don’t matter.

This looks the ID up in IntegrAuth’s own certificate records and shows what we issued against it — so it proves the certificate exists here and that the name, score and date match what we hold. It is not a proctored result: the Academy’s final exam is unsupervised — the learner answers it in their own browser and it is graded on our servers. Nothing you type is stored or used for anything besides this lookup.
